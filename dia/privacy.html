Dia / diaclassist

Privacy policy

Effective October 18, 2026

Dia is a private, single-owner personal assistant. This policy explains how its Google Calendar integration and related assistant records are used.

1. Application and contact

The Google OAuth application named diaclassist is used by Dia. It is not offered for public registration. Privacy questions can be sent to assistantkwong@gmail.com.

2. Information accessed

Google authorization supplies basic account identity, including the authorized email address, and permits access to Google calendars created by the application. Within those calendars, Dia can read Calendar metadata and event information and create, update or delete representations under the owner's authorized synchronization policy.

Event information may include titles, descriptions, times, timezones, locations, recurrence details, status and availability. Dia also keeps native activities, source evidence, synchronization receipts and relevant operation history in its private environment.

The Calendar authorization uses openid, userinfo.email and calendar.app.created. It does not grant access to Gmail message contents.

3. How information is used

Information is used to identify the authorized account, synchronize the app-created Calendar, reconcile event identities, and support the owner's planning and assistant features. Original evidence and operation history support investigation, duplicate prevention and recovery.

Dia does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Access is not offered to unrelated users.

4. Processing, storage and sharing

Assistant records and credentials are held in the owner's privately managed environment. Dia uses owner-controlled AI services for assistant processing. Google receives the information needed to carry out authorized Calendar operations. Access for administration or investigation remains subject to the owner's authorization.

Google user data is not used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Dia's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

5. Retention and deletion

Dia retains assistant records, original evidence, receipts and protected backups for the owner's ongoing use, audit and recovery. It does not apply a fixed automatic deletion period to all records.

The owner controls retention and may request deletion or review through the contact address above. Disconnecting Google authorization does not itself erase existing local records, backups or Calendar events; deletion of those records is a separate owner-controlled action.

6. Revoking Google access

The owner can revoke the application's access in Google Account third-party connections. Once the revocation takes effect, Dia cannot continue using that authorization. The owner can also stop synchronization through Dia's private administration.

7. This public website

This is a static informational website. It has no analytics scripts, sign-in form, tracking pixels or connection to Dia's private databases. No correspondence, Calendar contents or credentials are published here.

When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in GitHub's Pages documentation and GitHub's privacy statement. External links and email links operate only when selected.

8. Changes

This policy will be updated when the application's data practices change. The effective date above identifies this version.
