Google application identity

Dia / diaclassist

The Google OAuth application is named diaclassist. Its existing authorization requests basic account identity and access to app-created Google calendars.

This website explains the application. It does not provide a sign-in form, connect to Dia's private systems, or collect email or Calendar contents.
